Vela — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

The short version

Vela collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and no server. Everything you write, choose, or manifest stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your name, chosen focus areas, and ritual preferences
  • Your intentions and the stories generated from them
  • Journal entries, gratitude notes, and 3·6·9 sheets
  • Vision board goals and any photos you pin (copied into the app's private storage; never uploaded anywhere)
  • Your streak history and settings

Stories are generated entirely on your device. Nothing you type is sent to any server, including ours — we don't operate one.

Purchases

Vela Plus is processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. Purchase state is stored on your device; Restore Purchases uses Apple's own records.

Notifications

Ritual reminders are scheduled locally on your device. There is no push notification infrastructure and no server involved.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything, because everything lives inside it.
