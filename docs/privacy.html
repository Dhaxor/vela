Vela — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026 (Vela 1.1)

The short version

Vela collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and no server. Everything you write, choose, or photograph in Vela stays on your iPhone, unless you export a backup file and send it somewhere yourself.

What stays on your device

  • Your name, how the narrator says it, your focus areas, ritual times and listening preferences
  • Your dreams in your own words, and every chapter written from them
  • The steps you choose and the proofs you chart: each one-sentence proof, its date and its kind
  • Photos you add to a proof or to Scenes. Vela copies them into its own private storage on your iPhone; they are never uploaded anywhere
  • Journal pages (Script, 3·6·9 and Gratitude), your Scenes and their captions, the names you give your constellations
  • The nights you have shown up, your weekly rhythm and any pause

Chapters are written entirely on your device. Nothing you type is sent to any server, including ours: we don't operate one.

Backups you export

Settings → Your data → Export a backup makes a file with your dreams, chapters, steps, proofs, journal pages, Scenes captions, progress and settings. Photos are not inside it. Vela hands the file to the iOS share sheet, and it goes only where you send it (for example Files, iCloud Drive or an email to yourself). Anyone who can open that file can read what is in it, so keep it somewhere private.

Import a backup reads a file you choose and either merges it with what is on your iPhone or replaces it. Nothing is read without you picking the file.

Your iPhone's own backups

From Vela 1.1, Vela's data is included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or an encrypted Finder backup), so restoring a new iPhone from a backup brings your sky with it. Those backups belong to your Apple Account and are managed by Apple; Vela never receives a copy.

Purchases

Vela Plus is processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. Purchase state is stored on your device; Restore Purchases uses Apple's own records.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone at your ritual times. There is no push notification infrastructure and no server involved. "Keep my words off the Lock Screen" is on by default: reminders then use neutral words, and your dream and proofs never appear on a locked screen. You can turn it off in Settings if you would like reminders to quote you.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything Vela keeps on your iPhone. Backup files you exported are yours and are not touched: delete them from wherever you saved or sent them. Copies inside your iPhone's iCloud or Finder backups are removed when you delete those backups.
