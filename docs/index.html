Vela — Support

Vela is a manifestation journal that remembers what you actually do: a chapter written on your device from your own words, one real step for today, and your proof woven into the next chapter as a star in your own night sky. Unlimited listens are free forever, and everything stays private on your iPhone.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. There is nothing to sign up for, ever.
  • Does it work offline? Completely. Chapters are written on your device.
  • How do I move Vela to a new iPhone? On the old phone, open Settings → Your data → Export a backup and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive (or send it to yourself). On the new phone, open Settings → Your data → Import a backup and choose that file.
  • How do I restore Vela Plus? Settings → Restore purchases, or the Restore link on the Plus page.
  • How do I delete my data? Delete the app: everything Vela keeps lives inside it. Backup files you exported are yours, so delete those wherever you saved them.
  • Can I keep my words off the Lock Screen? That is the default. Settings → Lock Screen privacy controls whether reminders may quote you.
