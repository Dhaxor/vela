Vela — Support

Vela is the manifestation app that doesn't hold your practice hostage: instant on-device stories written from your own words, unlimited listens free forever, a daily ritual, the 3·6·9 method, and a vision board — all private by architecture.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. There is nothing to sign up for, ever.
  • Does it work offline? Completely. Stories are generated on your device.
  • How do I restore Vela Plus? Settings → Restore purchases, or the Restore link on the Plus page.
  • How do I delete my data? Delete the app — everything lives inside it.
