# Guideline 4.3(a) response — Vela Future Memory Studio

Thank you for reviewing Vela. This build is a product-level rebuild created specifically in response to the Guideline 4.3(a) concern. Vela is not a template, reskin, quote collection, or static manifestation-content app.

Vela now centres on an original closed-loop system called **Future Memory**:

1. The user names one desired future in their own words, the feeling attached to it and, optionally, one thing they have already done toward it.
2. Vela writes an on-device, personalised chapter from those words. Anything the user has already done is woven into it, highlighted in gold and tagged “Woven from your proof”.
3. The chapter ends with three specific, observable bridges for that day (two minutes, fifteen minutes, or brave). The user commits to one.
4. The user records whether they did it, something moved, or they learned something, with one sentence of evidence. “Not today” is also recorded, as a lesson.
5. That evidence becomes a star in the user's own constellation and is woven into the next chapter. Seven stars complete a season, which the user names.

This means the content is neither a fixed library nor a repeating daily visualisation. The story state changes in response to what the user actually does. The product’s core value is the continuing relationship between imagination, behaviour, evidence, and adaptive narrative.

Additional implementation details:

- all generation and storage are on-device; no account, server, feed, tracking, or connection is required;
- prior scenes are tracked to reduce repetition;
- narrated playback uses the device voice;
- proof is presented without scores, punishment, or public comparison;
- every chapter, bridge and proof is kept in an on-device Memory Book (the Arc tab);
- the Rituals tab (Script prompts linked to the user's own chapter, the 3·6·9 practice, gratitude), identity lines born from the user's proofs, and private Scenes are supporting practices rather than the primary product;
- the three optional soundscapes are original procedural audio generated for Vela;
- Vela has its own bundle identifier (`app.vela.manifest`), local data model, navigation, visual system, source code, screenshots, icon, and in-app purchase products.

## Suggested review path (Vela 1.1)

1. Complete the six short onboarding steps: choose an area, write any desired future in your own words, choose a feeling, a name and a ritual time. On the last step, **One thing you've already done toward this**, enter one sentence (for example “Bought the domain name.”) and select **Weave Chapter I**.
2. Chapter I opens. It uses your name and words, and the sentence from step 1 appears in a gold paragraph tagged **Woven from your proof**.
3. At the end of Chapter I, pick one of the three bridges and select **Make it observable**.
4. Select **I crossed it**, choose a proof type, enter one sentence, and select **Chart this proof**. The proof becomes a new star in your constellation.
5. Select **Carry it forward**. Chapter II opens, and its gold **Woven from your proof** paragraph contains the sentence entered in step 4, demonstrating that lived behaviour changes the product output.
6. On Today, the constellation shows both stars; tap it to open the sky and any star's sentence. The Arc tab keeps every chapter, bridge and proof in order.
