No login is required. Vela has no accounts, sign-in or servers. Everything is written and stored on the device, and the app works offline.

Vela is built around one loop. The app writes a chapter on the device from the user's own words, the user commits to one real step for today, then records one sentence of proof, and the next chapter is woven from that proof. Each proof becomes a star in the user's constellation.

TWO-MINUTE REVIEW PATH (fresh install)
1. Tap "Begin". Choose an area (for example Career) and tap "Continue".
2. On "In your own words.", type a few words or tap a suggestion, choose a feeling, and tap "Continue".
3. Enter a first name and tap "Continue". Choose Morning, Evening or Both and tap "Continue".
4. On "One thing you've already done toward this.", type one sentence, for example: Bought the domain name. Tap "Weave Chapter I".
5. A short weave animation (tap to skip) opens Chapter I. Scroll down: a gold paragraph tagged "Woven from your proof" quotes the sentence from step 4.
6. At the end of the chapter, under "Today's bridge", choose one bridge (2 minutes, 15 minutes or Brave) and tap "Make it observable". On iPhone, Vela may offer one reminder ("Remind me at…" or "Not now"). Either answer is fine.
7. Tap "I crossed it". On "What became real?", choose "I did it", type one sentence (for example: Sent my portfolio to two studios I admire.) and tap "Chart this proof".
8. Star Birth: the proof is charted as a new star ("Proof 2 · Charted"). Tap "Carry it forward".
9. Chapter II opens after a short weave. Its gold "Woven from your proof" paragraph quotes the sentence from step 7, so what the user did changes what the app writes.
10. Tap Back twice (Chapter II, then Chapter I) to reach Today. Today shows "2 stars". Tap the sky ("Open sky") to see the constellation, then tap a star to read its sentence. The Arc tab keeps every chapter, bridge and proof in order.

NARRATION
In any chapter, the round play button in the middle of the bottom dock reads the chapter aloud using the voices installed on the iPhone (the system speech synthesizer, through expo-speech). The audio is generated on the device. Nothing is uploaded, and no recorded or cloned voice is used. Voice, speed, sleep timer and soundscape are in the same dock. The soundscapes are original audio files bundled with the app. Background audio is used only while a chapter is playing.

PURCHASES
The full loop, the sky, unlimited listening and backup are free. Vela Plus (monthly, yearly or lifetime) adds more than one dream at a time, three extra soundscapes, every affirmation area and unlimited Scenes. "Restore purchases" is at the bottom of the paywall, next to "Terms of Use" and "Privacy Policy". It is also in Settings (the gear on Today), in the Vela Plus section. To open the paywall, go to Settings > "Vela Plus", or the Arc tab > "See Vela Plus".
